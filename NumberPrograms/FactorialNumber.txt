 Question: Find the Factorial of a Number

Explanation:

The factorial of a number is the product of all positive integers from 1 up to that number.

It is represented by the symbol `!`.

Formula:

n! = n × (n-1) × (n-2) × ... × 1

- Multiply all positive integers from the given number down to 1.
- The factorial of 0 is always 1.
- The factorial of 1 is also 1.

Example:

Number = 5

Factorial = 5 × 4 × 3 × 2 × 1

Factorial = 120

Test Cases:

1. Number = 5 → Output = 120
2. Number = 4 → Output = 24
3. Number = 6 → Output = 720
4. Number = 3 → Output = 6
5. Number = 0 → Output = 1
