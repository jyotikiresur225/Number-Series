STRONG NUMBER

Question

Write a program to check whether a given number is a Strong Number.

Explanation

A Strong Number is a number in which the sum of the factorials of its individual digits is equal to the original number.

The factorial of a number is the product of all positive integers from 1 up to that number. The factorial of 0 is 1.

For example, 145 is a Strong Number because the sum of the factorials of its digits is equal to 145.

Logic

1. Take the given number as input.
2. Store the original number in a separate variable.
3. Initialize a variable named sum to 0.
4. Extract the last digit of the number.
5. Calculate the factorial of the extracted digit.
6. Add the factorial to sum.
