# Question: Find the Area of a Rectangle

**Explanation:**

The area of a rectangle is calculated by multiplying its length by its breadth (width).

**Formula:**

Area = Length × Breadth

- Length represents one side of the rectangle.
- Breadth represents the other side of the rectangle.
- Multiply both values to get the area in square units.

**Example:**

Length = 5

Breadth = 3

Area = 5 × 3 = 15 square units.

**Test Cases:**

1. Length = 5, Breadth = 3 → Output = 15
2. Length = 10, Breadth = 5 → Output = 50
3. Length = 7, Breadth = 4 → Output = 28
4. Length = 12, Breadth = 6 → Output = 72
5. Length = 8, Breadth = 8 → Output = 64
