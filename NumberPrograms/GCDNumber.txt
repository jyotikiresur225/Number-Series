Question: Find the GCD of Two Numbers

Explanation:

GCD (Greatest Common Divisor) is the largest positive number that divides two given numbers without leaving a remainder.

It is also known as HCF (Highest Common Factor).

Logic:

1. Read two numbers.
2. Find the remainder when the first number is divided by the second number.
3. Replace the first number with the second number.
4. Replace the second number with the remainder.
5. Repeat the process until the remainder becomes zero.
6. When the remainder becomes zero, the first number is the GCD.

Example:

Input: 12, 18

Calculation:

18 % 12 = 6

12 % 6 = 0

Output: GCD = 6

Test Cases:

1. Input: 12, 18
   Output: GCD = 6

2. Input: 20, 30
   Output: GCD = 10

3. Input: 15, 25
   Output: GCD = 5

4. Input: 7, 13
   Output: GCD = 1

5. Input: 24, 36
   Output: GCD = 12
