Question: Find the LCM of Two Numbers

Explanation:

LCM (Least Common Multiple) is the smallest positive number that is exactly divisible by two given numbers without leaving a remainder.

Logic:

1. Read two numbers.
2. Find the greater number between them.
3. Start checking multiples from the greater number.
4. Check whether the current number is divisible by both given numbers.
5. If it is divisible by both numbers, it is the LCM.
6. Otherwise, increment the number and repeat the process until a common multiple is found.

Example:

Input: 12, 18

Multiples of 12: 12, 24, 36, 48...

Multiples of 18: 18, 36, 54...

The smallest common multiple is 36.

Output: LCM = 36

Test Cases:

1. Input: 12, 18
   Output: LCM = 36

2. Input: 4, 6
   Output: LCM = 12

3. Input: 5, 10
   Output: LCM = 10

4. Input: 7, 13
   Output: LCM = 91

5. Input: 8, 12
   Output: LCM = 24
