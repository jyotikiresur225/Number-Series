SPECIAL TWO DIGIT NUMBER

Question

Write a program to check whether a given two digit number is a Special Two Digit Number.

Explanation

A Special Two Digit Number is a number in which the sum of its digits and the product of its digits, when added together, is equal to the original number.

For example, 59 is a Special Two Digit Number because the sum of its digits is 14 and the product of its digits is 45. Adding them gives 59.

Logic

1. Take a two digit number as input.
2. Extract the tens digit by dividing the number by 10.
3. Extract the units digit by finding the remainder when the number is divided by 10.
4. Calculate the sum of both digits.
5. Calculate the product of both digits.
6. Add the sum and product.
7. Compare the result with the original number.
8. If both are equal, the number is a Special Two Digit Number. Otherwise, it is not.

Formula

Sum = Tens digit + Units digit

Product = Tens digit multiplied by Units digit

Final Result = Sum + Product
