Question: Find the Circumference of a Circle

Explanation:

The circumference of a circle is the total distance around its boundary.

It is calculated by multiplying 2 by π (pi) and the radius of the circle.

Formula:

Circumference = 2 × π × Radius

- Radius is the distance from the centre of the circle to its boundary.
- π (pi) is approximately 3.14.
- Multiply 2, π, and the radius to find the circumference.

Example:

Radius = 5

Circumference = 2 × 3.14 × 5

Circumference = 31.4 units.

Test Cases:

1. Radius = 5 → Output = 31.4
2. Radius = 7 → Output = 43.96
3. Radius = 10 → Output = 62.8
4. Radius = 2 → Output = 12.56
5. Radius = 1 → Output = 6.28
