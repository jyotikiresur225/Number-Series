Question: Find the Perimeter of a RectangleExplanation:

The perimeter of a rectangle is the total distance around its boundary.

It is calculated by adding the lengths of all four sides of the rectangle.

Formula:

Perimeter = 2 × (Length + Breadth)

- Length represents the longer side of the rectangle.
- Breadth represents the shorter side of the rectangle.
- Add the length and breadth, then multiply the result by 2 to find the perimeter.

Example:

Length = 5

Breadth = 3

Perimeter = 2 × (5 + 3)

Perimeter = 2 × 8 = 16 units.

Test Cases:

1. Length = 5, Breadth = 3 → Output = 16
2. Length = 10, Breadth = 5 → Output = 30
3. Length = 7, Breadth = 4 → Output = 22
4. Length = 12, Breadth = 6 → Output = 36
5. Length = 8, Breadth = 8 → Output = 32
